Based on the backend security configurations (@PreAuthorize), the system is designed with a very clear hierarchy. Here is exactly what each role can do:

👑 ADMIN
The Admin has the highest level of access and can do everything HR and Employees can do, plus:

Department Management: Only the Admin has the authority to create, update, or delete Departments across the company.
👔 HR (Human Resources)
HR has full operational control over the workforce and company data, but cannot modify the core structural departments. Their permissions include:

Employee Management: Can add new employees, update profiles, and delete employees.
Payroll & Finance: Can process payroll and generate salary slips.
Announcements: Can create, edit, or delete company-wide announcements.
Dashboard Access: Has exclusive access to the HR metrics dashboard to view headcount and analytics.
Document Management: Can upload and manage compliance or personal documents for employees.
Leave & Performance (Shared with Managers): Can approve/reject leave applications and submit performance reviews.
👤 EMPLOYEE (Self-Service)
Employees have restricted access. They can only view public company information or manage their own personal data:

Attendance: Can clock in and clock out daily, and view their own attendance history.
Leave Applications: Can apply for leave (Sick, Casual, etc.) and check the status of their requests.
View Personal Records: Can view their own payslips, read their performance reviews, and access their own documents.
View Company Data: Can read company announcements, view the list of departments, and view basic employee directory profiles.
(Note: There is also a MANAGER role in the code that acts as a middle-ground. Managers can approve leaves, write performance reviews, and view the list of employees, but they cannot process payroll or create company-wide announcements).
